Whisper is an input method editor (IME) with voice recognition based on Whisper

It can work as standalone app and also offers an IME which can e.g. be activated
via the microphone button in <a href="https://f-droid.org/packages/helium314.keyboard/">HeliBoard</a>.

At first start it needs to download the Whisper models from Hugging Face.
This is the only use of the internet permission.

Voice recognition works completely offline.

As standalone app Whisper can also translate any supported language to English.

Floating microphone (optional): a button appears next to any text field. Tap it to dictate and
insert the text without switching keyboards. It uses an accessibility service only to find the
focused text field and to insert the text. Nothing is read or stored, audio never leaves the device.

Custom words: add names and terms that Whisper gets wrong (e.g. "cloud code = Claude Code").

To get the most out of Whisper:
<ul>
<li>Tap the button to start and stop recording, or press and hold it while speaking</li>
<li>Pause briefly before starting to speak</li>
<li>Speak clearly, loudly, and at a moderate pace</li>
<li>Recordings can be up to 5 minutes long</li>
</ul>
